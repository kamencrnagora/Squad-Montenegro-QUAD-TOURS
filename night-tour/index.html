Good to know

  • Drivers: minimum age 21 and a valid driving licence is required.
  • Passengers: from 12 years old. Children can ride as passengers.
  • Equipment: helmet and protective gear are included in the price.
  • Weight: there is no weight limit.
  • Booking: we operate by booking. There are no special booking conditions, just contact us.
  • Bad weather: by agreement with you, the tour can be cancelled in good time.
  • Transport: we offer a transport option for certain tours. Ask us when you book.
