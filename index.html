Frequently asked questions

What is included in the price?

All tours include snacks, drinks, a hot espresso, and helmet and protective gear. The Big Boyz Tour also includes a meal at a restaurant.

Do you offer any discounts?

Yes. We offer a 10% discount if you book 2 or more quads. You also get 10% off at partner businesses such as Locanda restaurant in Perast, and 10% off every tour when you stay at a partner accommodation. See the partner programme.

Which tour is best for me?

If you are short on time or a beginner, The Vista View is a great start. For the most complete experience with the best off-road sections and lush vegetation, we recommend the Big Boyz Tour.

Do I need experience to drive the quad?

Not necessarily. Our shorter routes are designed to be accessible, while our longest routes feature more technical off-road sections for those seeking a challenge.

Who can drive and who can ride as a passenger?

Drivers must be at least 21 years old and hold a valid driving licence. Passengers can be from 12 years old, so children can ride with you. There is no weight limit.

What should I wear?

Comfortable outdoor clothing and closed-toe shoes. We climb to high altitudes, so it can be cooler at the summit than in Risan. A light jacket is a good idea. Helmet and protective gear are provided.

Is lunch provided?

The Big Boyz Tour includes a meal at a restaurant. All tours include snacks and drinks.

How long do the tours last?

From about 2.5 hours for The Vista View to a full-day Big Boyz Tour with no time limit. Times are approximate and depend on weather, road conditions and participants.

Do you run night tours?

Yes. Night tours are the same as our day tours. Ask us for a night departure when you book. See the night tour page.

How do I book?

Send us a message on WhatsApp, an email or the inquiry form. We operate by booking and there are no special booking conditions.

What if the weather is bad?

By agreement with you, the tour can be cancelled in good time if weather conditions are bad.

Do you offer transport?

For certain tours we offer a transport option. Ask us when you book.

Safety first

Your guide will give you a full safety briefing before departure. Helmets and protective equipment are provided, and all participants must follow the guide's instructions throughout the tour.

Important safety information

Riding safely

  • Quads handle differently from cars. On uneven or steep terrain they can tip, even at moderate speeds, so speed should always match the terrain.
  • Surface matters. Stopping distances are longer on gravel, rocks and wet surfaces.
  • Ask if you are unsure. Quads feel easy to ride, but distance, speed and braking are easy to misjudge. Your guide is there to help.
  • Weather and terrain change conditions. Rain, mud, loose gravel and slopes call for a slower pace.
  • Wear your protection. Always wear the helmet and protective equipment provided. Riding responsibly is the most important safety measure.

Terms of participation

  • Mountain terrain: some sections run along steep mountain edges, so staying focused and on the designated trail is important.
  • Guide's instructions: please follow your guide's directions at all times. Leaving the designated trail or riding recklessly is at your own risk.
  • Fitness and sobriety: participants confirm they are in good physical health and not under the influence of alcohol or any substances that may impair judgement.
  • Inherent risk: quad riding is an adventure activity that carries inherent risks, including the risk of injury.

By participating in this tour, you accept these risks and release the organizer from liability in the event of an accident.
